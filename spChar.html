HTML Special Characters

Entity: a mechanism for writing special characters or symbols in HTML, such as mathematical symbols, characters in most languages, and many other symbols. Many HTML entities can be specified by name, by a decimal number, or by a hexadecimal number.

Entities for reserved characters

The < and & characters can be displayed useing entities. &lt; for less than, and &amp; for the ampersand. Ex. 2 < 3 = True

Non-Breaking Characters

A non-breaking character is an inter-word character that permits treating the words on both sides to be one word

Non-Breaking Hyphen

A non-Breaking Hyphen, ‑, looks like a regular hyphen but acts like a normal character in the middle of a world. Non‑breaking hyphen.
