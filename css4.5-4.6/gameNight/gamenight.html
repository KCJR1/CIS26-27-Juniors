About the Event

Once a quarter, the Computer & Information Science program turns the lab into an arcade. Students bring their favorite games, set up the stations, and run the tournament brackets themselves. It is a chance to practice event planning, networking, and troubleshooting, and to have some fun after a long week.

Every station is built and configured by CIS students. If a console will not connect, a student tech is on call to fix it.

STAFF NOTE: Remember to reserve the lab and order the pizza by Monday.

House Rules

Treat the equipment the way you would treat your own. Every console, cable, and controller is shared by the whole program, so report anything broken right away instead of trying to hide it.

Good sportsmanship is required. Shake hands after every match, cheer for every player, and keep the trash talk friendly. The tournament director's call is final.
